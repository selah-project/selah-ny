# Kuthandiza ndi kukonza kumasulira kwa Chichewa kwa Selah

Zikomo. Uku ndi kumasulira koyamba; muli zolakwika, ndipo kunena
cholakwika ndi thandizo lenileni. Simufunika kukhala katswiri: nenani
chimene mwaona, perekani zifukwa zanu, ndipo siyanitsani chimene
muchidziwa ndi chimene mukungoganiza.

## Nenani kapena perekani lingaliro

- Ngati liwu likufuna kukambirana, kapena pali kuwerenga kopitirira
  kumodzi — tsegulani **Issue**.
- Ngati cholakwika ndi kukonza kwake zonse zili zoonekeratu —
  tsegulani **Pull request**.
- Pa mafunso a pulogalamu kapena a chitetezo — [Selah
  Support](https://selahproject.com/support).

## Chimene sichisinthidwa

Mafayilo ali m'mtundu `<buku>/<chaputala>/<vesi>.json`.

**Gawo la `surface` si lathu.** Ndi pamwamba — mbiri ya Chihebri.
Ngati mupeza pamenepo chinthu chimene si zilembo za Chihebri —
zilembo za Chilatini kapena za zinenero zina, madontho, mivi — ndi
kuwonongeka; nenani, koma musakonze mwa kuganiza. Mtundu wolondola
umachokera ku maziko.

**Chiwerengero cha matanthauzo n'chofanana ndi chiwerengero cha mawu a
Chihebri.** Musaphatikize matokeni awiri kukhala tanthauzo limodzi,
ndipo musagawe tanthauzo limodzi.

**`book`, `chapter`, `verse`, `ref`**, ndi ndondomeko ya matokeni —
sizisinthidwa. Kuthyola kugwirizana kwa tokeni ndi tanthauzo lake ndi
cholakwika chachikulu kwambiri.

**⟨את⟩ sichimasuliridwa ndipo sichichotsedwa.** Ndi liwu limene lili
m'malemba. Silembedwa monga mwa mamvekedwe (osati ⟨et⟩).

Ngati kusintha kukukhudza zonse ziwiri — konzani `translation` ndi
`gloss` yake pamodzi.

## Cholakwika chachikulu choyamba: manambala

Chichewa chimamanga manambala 6–9 pa **zisanu**: *zisanu ndi chimodzi*
ndi 6, *zisanu ndi ziwiri* ndi 7, *zisanu ndi zitatu* ndi 8, *zisanu
ndi zinayi* ndi 9. Makina analakwitsa apa kuposa kulikonse, ndipo apa
ndi pamene diso la munthu limathandiza kwambiri.

- **Tsiku la Sabata ndi tsiku lachisanu ndi chiwiri** (Genesis 2:2–3;
  Eksodo 20:11) — osati lachisanu ndi chimodzi. Masiku a ntchito ndi
  **asanu ndi limodzi**.
- **מאתים ndi mazana awiri** (200), osati makumi awiri (20). **עשרים
  ndi makumi awiri** (20), osati khumi ndi ziwiri (12).
- Nambala ya m'vesi lonse iyenera kufanana ndi nambala ya m'mizere ya
  matokeni. Werengani mizere, kenako werengani vesi.
- Zaka za makolo (Genesis 5; Genesis 11) ndi manambala a kalembera
  (Numeri 1–4; 26) ndi malo abwino kwambiri oyesera: werengani
  mbadwo ndi mbadwo ndi diso lanu.

Ngati mupeza nambala imene sikugwirizana ndi Chihebri, nenani — ngakhale
kuti simukudziwa mtundu wolondola wa Chichewa. Kunena kokha
n'kothandiza.

## Cholakwika chachiwiri: chinenero cha mnansi

Chichewa chili ndi anansi amene tikuwamvetsa, ndipo apa pali ngozi
yaikulu. Makina nthawi zina amaterera kupita ku Chiswahili kapena ku
Chitumbuka pakati pa vesi — ndipo popeza chotulukapo chimaoneka ngati
chimveka, n'kosavuta kusachiona.

**Chichewa chilibe chilembo chakecho.** Zilembo za Chilatini
zimagawidwa ndi anansi ake; sizitiuza chinenero. Chimene chimauza ndi
**mawu athunthu**.

| Chiswahili | Chichewa |
|---|---|
| `lakini` | koma |
| `sasa` | tsopano |
| `pia` | komanso, ndiponso |
| `hapana` | ayi |
| `mtu` / `watu` | munthu / anthu |
| `nchi` | dziko |
| `maji` | madzi |
| `mbingu` | kumwamba, thambo |
| `mfalme` | mfumu |
| `neno` | mawu, liwu |
| `alisema` / `akasema` | anati, adati |
| `siku` | tsiku |
| `nuru` / `giza` | kuwala / mdima |

**Chitumbuka:** `kweni` (koma), `charu` (dziko), `wakati` /
`wakayowoya` (anati), `ŵanthu` (anthu). **Chingerezi:** `the`, `and`,
`of`, `Lord`, `LORD`, `God`.

**Mawu ogawana sanenedwa:** *na* mkati mwa verebu (*anapita*),
**kama** (bedi), **mkono**, **bwana** (mbuye wa munthu),
**kwenikweni**, **ndiyo**, ndi *nyumba, mwana, moto, madzi* — ndi mawu
a Chichewa, ngakhale akhale m'Chiswahili nawonso. Yesani mawu
athunthu, osati zidutswa za mawu.

**Musakonze mwa kusintha liwu limodzi lokha.** Pamene galamala ya
chinenero china yalowa m'vesi, kusintha liwu limodzi kumasiya
chiganizo chimene sichili cha chinenero chimodzi. **Kukonza kumene
kungapange chinthu chimene chinenero chilibe sikukonza.** Vesi
lotero limamasuliridwa kwatsopano lonse.

## Mkati mwa ⟨ ⟩ mulibe chinenero chachilendo

Mkati mwa mabulaketi muli Chichewa kapena chizindikiro cha Chihebri
⟨את⟩. Ngati muli Chingerezi pamenepo, ndi cholakwika.

## Mawu a mwambo m'malo mwa Dzina

Pamene tokeni ya Chihebri ikuti יהוה, payenera kukhala **Yahwe** —
osati *Yehova*, osati *Chauta*, osati *Ambuye*, osati *Mulungu*.
Pamene ikuti אלהים monga Dzina, payenera kukhala **Elohim**. Pamene
ikuti אדני monga Dzina, payenera kukhala **Adonayi**.

Yang'anani tsinde, osati mtundu umodzi wokha: *Yehova*, *wa Yehova*,
*kwa Ambuye*, *wa Mulungu* — onse.

**Koma tokeni ya Chihebri ndiyo imaweruza, osati liwu la Chichewa.**
*Mulungu*, *milungu* ndi ovomerezeka pa milungu ya amitundu; *mbuye*
ndi wovomerezeka pamene munthu akunena kwa munthu. Musanene liwu
lokha; nenani awiriwo *tokeni + tanthauzo*.

## Kugwira ntchito ndi AI

Ngati mwagwiritsa ntchito modeli yaikulu ya chinenero kapena
kumasulira kwa makina — nenani momveka, pamodzi ndi momwe
mwayesera ntchitoyo. Musatumize mavesi ambiri osayesedwa.

## Momwe mungauze cholakwika

Nenani malo, liwu la Chihebri, ndi chimene chili pamenepo:

```
psalms/33/12 — surface יהוה, tanthauzo "Ambuye"; malinga ndi tebulo la Dzina liyenera kukhala Yahwe
```

Malo ayenera kukhala ndi **chaputala**. Popanda chaputala, palibe amene
angapeze chimene mwapeza.

## Kudziwa kale n'kwabwino

Kumasulira uku sikosalala. Pamene Chihebri chili chovuta, kumakhala
chovuta. Pamene chili ndi matanthauzo ambiri, sichisankha m'malo mwa
wowerenga. Malo ovuta kuwerenga nthawi zambiri ndi pamene kumasulira
kukugwira ntchito yake — osati pamene kwalephera.

## Makhalidwe

Khalani woona, khalani wokoma mtima, onetsani maumboni anu.
Siyanitsani chimene muchidziwa ndi chimene mukupereka monga lingaliro.
Osamalira amayesa ndipo amagamula.
